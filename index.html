How It Works

Three steps to your medicine.

From tap to doorstep — effortless, transparent, and blazing fast.

Step 01

Place Your Order

Search from 1,200+ medicines, upload a prescription, or hit the SOS emergency button. One tap is all it takes.

Step 02

Pharmacy Dispatches

The nearest verified pharmacy receives your order instantly, packs it, and hands it to our rider within minutes.

Step 03

Delivered to Your Door

Track your rider live on the map. Your medicine arrives sealed, safe, and on time — every single time.

🚨 Emergency Mode

Medibee's Emergency Mode dispatches your critical medicines in under 8 minutes. No waiting, no delays — just speed.
